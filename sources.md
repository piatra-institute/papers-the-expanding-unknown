## Provenance exclusions

The private seed conversation is not a citable source and stays in ignored chats/. Secondary search results were navigation aids only. Unverified metaphysical claims about “ingressing minds,” claims that schools invariably extinguish curiosity, and claims that bacteria or organs experience curiosity are not imported into the manuscript.

Downloaded source copies and OCR intermediates were kept outside the repository in a temporary research directory. They are not redistributed with the paper. Claims about the proposed model and study are the paper's own definitions, assumptions and interpretations, identified as such in claims.yaml.
