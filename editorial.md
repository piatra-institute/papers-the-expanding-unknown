# Editorial record

18 October 2026. Argument, structure and voice review completed; the machine-readable sign-off identifies the final manuscript. PDF inspection is recorded separately against the final output hash. Review was by the authoring agent, not an external peer reviewer.

## Contribution and reader

The paper addresses a reader who wants to know what it means to say that learning encourages curiosity. Its contribution is a distinction among candidate availability, practical costs and valuation/motivation, with an explicit identification problem and a prospective study. It is not a new universal theory of curiosity. The opening repair is a hypothetical illustration, not fieldwork or evidence.

The central claim is a conditional possibility: more recognizable opportunities can sustain inquiry under an unchanged pursuit rule. The finite-set accounting identity and branching construction make that possibility explicit. The study explains what would be required to investigate it in people. It remains unperformed.

## Material worth preserving

- The repair gives the reader a concrete reason to distinguish an answer from the next question.
- The opportunity definition excludes willingness, avoiding a circular explanation of increased search.
- The snapshot count does not silently include questions generated later in its observation window.
- The distinction between new composition and accessible retrieval limits what question elicitation can establish.
- The study's common-menu condition is useful precisely because its limitations are stated: supplying a menu can itself teach, reduce effort and signal approval.
- The return to the bicycle ends with the learner's next investigation. It avoids a general celebration of curiosity or a slogan about endless questioning.

## Reverse outline

1. **A repair that leaves questions:** establishes the behavioral ambiguity and states the limited contribution.
2. **What becomes available:** defines the candidate set, proves the expectation identity, separates search from reported curiosity, and addresses question identity, elicitation and entropy.
3. **How an answer can sustain inquiry:** demonstrates conditional expansion under fixed pursuit parameters; follows the derivation with convergence, failure and resource counterexamples.
4. **What earlier accounts already explain:** recognizes the information-gap, progress, network and question-reuse predecessors, then presents usefulness and reinforcement as serious rivals.
5. **A study that could distinguish the mechanisms:** turns the distinction into randomized contrasts, measurement requirements and disconfirming outcomes. Avoids post-treatment selection and a null-as-equivalence inference.
6. **Cheap information and the desire to obtain it:** uses the seed's artificial and non-neural cases to delimit the account. These cases do not take over its argument.
7. **What a lesson leaves open:** returns to the educational concern without condemning instruction in general; defends attention to independent investigation while retaining legitimate closure.

## Strongest rival

Learning may change the usefulness or reward value of already available questions rather than generate candidates. Moreover, Loewenstein already allows a shifting informational reference point; Patankar et al. explicitly model expanding/flexible representations; Liquin et al. study question reuse and recombination. These precedents rule out a claim of priority for generative inquiry. A sufficiently rich learning-progress or value model could incorporate candidate generation. The paper therefore argues for explanatory separation and appropriate measurement rather than a rival theory that supersedes those accounts.

## Scientific limits after review

The evidence review is selective, not systematic. Question accessibility remains indirectly measured. The model is an existence construction, not a fitted theory. The proposed experiment needs stimuli, pilots, preregistration, power/precision planning and appropriate research oversight before participant recruitment. No empirical result is supplied by the local verification gate. Publication and any external review remain separate decisions.
